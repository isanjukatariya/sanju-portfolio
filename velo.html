Overview

VELO is a campus mobility platform designed to make getting around university campuses smarter, greener, and more convenient. It allows students to discover, book, and rent EV cycles and scooters through a seamless digital experience.

From exploring available rides and booking time slots to making secure payments and tracking active rides, VELO brings the entire rental journey into one place. The goal was to create a student-friendly mobility experience that encourages sustainable transportation while making everyday campus commutes effortless.

Project Goal

Campus life is already a lot. Getting from your hostel to class shouldn't feel like a side quest.

The goal of VELO was to make everyday campus commutes simpler and more accessible by helping students discover, book, and rent EV cycles and scooters with ease.

The goal was to make the whole experience feel effortless, from discovering available rides and booking a time slot to making payments and tracking an ongoing ride.

But it wasn't just about getting students from point A to point B. VELO also aims to make sustainable mobility a more natural part of campus life, while helping university administrators manage their fleet, track usage, and keep everything running smoothly.

Less walking around. Less rental chaos. More time for the stuff that actually matters.
