Overview

E-Summit '26 is an event website designed to bring the energy of entrepreneurship, innovation, and startup culture into one engaging digital experience.

Built around the theme 'Ideas Beyond Boundaries', the website gives the event a distinct visual identity while helping visitors explore what the summit is all about. The design focuses on creating an experience that feels modern, immersive, and easy to navigate, balancing bold event visuals with clear content and intuitive interactions.

From communicating the event's identity to presenting essential information in an accessible way, the goal was to make the website feel like more than just an event page. It was designed as a digital introduction to the ideas, people and possibilities behind E-Summit '26.
