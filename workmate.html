Overview

WorkMate is a multilingual workforce management app designed to help employees easily access job-related information, manage daily tasks, and communicate in their preferred language.

Built with a mobile-first, accessibility-focused approach, the platform streamlines attendance tracking, leave requests, service desk tickets, and company announcements across English, Hindi, and regional languages so every worker feels confident navigating their workday.

Project Goal

Most enterprise workforce tools are built for desk workers first, leaving on-ground and multilingual teams struggling with complex menus and language barriers.

The goal of WorkMate was to strip away that friction by designing clear visual hierarchies, instant language switching, and intuitive daily workflows—from marking attendance and requesting leave to raising support tickets and checking shift updates.

Clear communication. Zero language friction. A workplace tool that actually works for everyone on the floor.
